# House style (Gled's standing preferences; applies to every film)

Add a line whenever Gled states a general preference. Newest at the bottom. Per-film direction goes in
`projects/<repo>/brief.md` instead.

- Audience: scientists outside my sub-field, managers, funders. Explain the ecological question before the method.
- Keep GitDiagram's visual template (lilac grid, serif headings, chips/boxes, karaoke captions). No GitDiagram branding.
- Lead with what the method lets you *know* (absolute abundance, maps, detection), not with the software.
- The joint model is the hero idea: two imperfect kinds of evidence, fitted together, so each fixes the other's blind spot.
- Be precise: never overstate results; no invented numbers; species and places as written in the repo.
- Reproducibility is a selling point: one script/project reproduces the paper's figures.
